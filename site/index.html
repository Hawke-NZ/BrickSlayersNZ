WHO ARE THESE PSYCHOS?

      
We're Brick Slayers NZ, a bricklaying crew out of South Auckland. We lay bricks. That's the whole personality.

      
Straight lines. Tight joints. Zero excuses. Every wall gets built like the neighbours are watching, because they are.

      
Other outfits will hand you a quote written in corporate beige. We'll build you a wall that slaps. Yeah nah, we'll send the quote too.

      
Our secret mortar mix is sand, cement, lime and spite. Kevin's real name is also Kevin.
